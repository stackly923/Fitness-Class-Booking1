Membership FAQs

Can I change my plan?

Yes. You can upgrade at any time and request a downgrade before your next renewal.

Do unused sessions roll over?

Active monthly sessions can roll forward for one billing cycle while your plan remains active.

Can I pause my membership?

Eligible plans can be paused for travel, recovery or major schedule changes.
