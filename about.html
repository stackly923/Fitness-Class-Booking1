From One Training Floor to a Connected Fitness Community

STACKLY Fitness started in 2018 with one studio, six coaches and a commitment to make expert-led group training feel personal.

Our founders saw that people did not need louder motivation—they needed clearer guidance, flexible times and a place where progress was measured without pressure. The first STACKLY timetable balanced strength, conditioning and recovery in one membership.

Today, our coaching team supports thousands of member journeys across studio and digital touchpoints. The scale has changed; the standard has not.
